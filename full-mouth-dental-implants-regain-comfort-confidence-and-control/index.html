Published May 19, 2025By Dr. Rose Magno

When faced with tooth loss, what concerns come to mind first? Do you worry about how it will affect your oral health? Do you anticipate the cost of replacing missing teeth? Or do you think about the frustrations of living with dentures? One thing is for certain—ignoring the problem won’t make it go away. In fact, doing so can wreak havoc on your well-being. But how do you get started? Learning about your options is a good start, and with today’s advancements in dentistry, there’s no shortage of tooth replacement solutions. If you’re missing all or most of your teeth, full mouth dental implants could be the relief you need!

At San Francisco East Bay Mini Dental Implants, Dr. Rose Magno offers denture alternatives like full mouth dental implants, providing patients like you with the relief they need to move forward.

The Key Ingredient: Mini Implants

There’s nothing quite as exciting as savoring a juicy steak or eating crunchy corn on the cob after experiencing tooth loss. But, wait—how can you do any of these things with missing teeth? Wouldn’t tooth loss make these eating endeavors significantly more difficult, if not impossible? Normally, it would. But not with full mouth dental implants! This tooth replacement option uses dental implants to replace an entire arch of missing teeth.

As a Mini Dental Implant Center of America, our office focuses on restoring smiles with mini dental implants. These innovative implants make advanced tooth replacement accessible to more people than ever before.

A mini dental implant is a small but powerful tooth replacement tool. The implant looks like a screw on one end with an O-ball head on the other. While the screw goes into your jawbone, the O-ball sits above the gums to secure your false teeth. This design differs from a standard implant, which has multiple, larger pieces. Thanks to the slim, single-piece construction of mini implants, Dr. Magno can place them in one minimally invasive procedure.

This gentle and efficient option makes mini implants a preferred choice for many patients. With less trauma to the mouth, you can expect an easier and quicker recovery. Plus, less time in the dental chair means fewer appointments and lower costs.

On top of these advantages, more people are eligible for mini implants than traditional ones. Because they take up less space in the jaw, many patients who have experienced bone loss can still qualify. But, if they’re so small, are they strong enough to support a full-arch restoration like a denture or a roundhouse bridge? Yes! The good news is that mini dental implants have comparable success rates to traditional implants. They allow you to eat all your favorite foods again, from pizza and steak to tacos and popcorn (and more)!

Full Mouth Dental Implants: What Are My Options?

If you need to replace all of your teeth and don’t want dentures (or are ready to upgrade!), full mouth dental implants are a great choice. They’re a secure and reliable way to transform your smile, restore your bite, and improve your oral health.

At our practice, Dr. Magno provides different implant systems so that you have an option that fits your lifestyle.

Snap-in dentures use 4-8 mini implants to support an entire denture. If you have dentures that are in good condition, Dr. Magno may be able to convert them into snap-in dentures. This option is removable, making it easy to clean your denture and snap it back onto your implants.

Fix-on-Six® is another option that some patients prefer for its aesthetic value and stability. This system uses a full-arch prosthetic called a roundhouse bridge. Unlike a denture, it eliminates the need for bulky acrylic gums. Made from high-quality materials, the bridge looks, feels, and functions more like natural teeth. Plus, you don’t have to worry about removing the bridge for cleaning. Only Dr. Magno will remove it during your cleaning appointments.

If you’re looking for a more permanent option, a fixed roundhouse bridge is a great choice. Dr. Magno cements this roundhouse onto 10-12 mini implants. It’s a permanent solution that neither you nor Dr. Magno will remove. This roundhouse functions the most like natural teeth. It boasts a natural appearance, increased chewing efficiency, and an unrivaled stability.

These advanced solutions offer you the support you need to live with confidence. Whether you’re eating, laughing, speaking, or smiling, your false teeth will stay securely in place.

Restore Your Smile With Mini Dental Implants!

Tooth loss can take away life’s simple joys, like enjoying a meal or laughing freely. But with full mouth dental implants, you no longer have to live with the discomfort or insecurity of missing teeth. Using mini dental implants, Dr. Magno can replace an entire row of teeth, restoring your smile’s look and function.

Just searching for “full mouth dental implants” is a big step. Now that you’ve made it here, we encourage you to take the next step and schedule a consultation with Dr. Magno. With her expert guidance, you’ll be well on your way to better oral health and renewed self-esteem.
