Published January 19, 2026By Dr. Rose Magno

Were you in a car crash, slip and fall, or other accident caused by someone else’s negligence? Are you in the middle of a personal injury case that caused damage to your teeth? After an accident, don’t suffer through the pain of damaged, broken, or missing teeth. Fix your smile sooner by calling an emergency dentist in San Francisco.

Emergency dentists have the skill, experience, and resources to treat you quickly. They focus on addressing tooth damage from trauma before it worsens. By providing urgent care, not only will an emergency dentist help ease pain and restore your smile, but they will also help produce important documentation that acts as evidence to build your personal injury claim.

Can’t afford emergency dentistry while you deal with a personal injury case? We’re happy to provide the essential care you need without requiring you to pay anything up front through a medical lien.

An Emergency Dentist in San Francisco Restores and Prevents More Damage

After an accident, you may have broken or cracked teeth. Or you might have knocked out a tooth altogether. Leaving these issues unaddressed leaves you in a lot of pain and discomfort. Damage also may worsen if you don’t treat it quickly. For instance, small cracks might deepen, or injuries might be infected. If you lose a tooth, the remaining teeth can shift, leading to misalignment of your bite.

An emergency dentist provides urgent dental care, helping ease your pain by restoring broken or missing teeth right away. At our office, Dr. Magno can restore chipped or cracked teeth using dental crowns. Or, when teeth have smaller cracks, she may repair them with dental bonding. And when there is too much damage to mend with other treatment options, she might extract the tooth and replace it with a mini dental implant.

When you come in for urgent dental care, Dr. Magno will develop an appropriate treatment plan for your injuries and damage. Once she reconstructs your damaged or missing teeth, you’ll leave the office with a restored smile that looks beautiful and feels even better. With efficient, high-quality emergency care, you’ll be able to get back to your life and overcome your injuries sooner.

Pay for Your Care and Document Injuries With a Medical Lien

No one expects to be in an accident. So it’s understandable if you weren’t financially prepared for unexpected dental repairs. And if you don’t have dental insurance, you may worry whether you can afford to treat your injuries at all. But with a medical lien, you’ll be able to access the care you need now.

At our office, Dr. Magno offers dental restoration through medical liens. As a dentist on lien, Dr. Magno agrees to treat you now without requiring upfront payment. Once you finalize your settlement, a portion of the final amount will be paid to us for the services provided.

But pursuing dental care through a medical lien doesn’t just remove financial barriers; it also strengthens your documentation for your personal injury case. Seeing an emergency dentist as soon as you sustain damage helps document your injuries and treatment through X-rays and appointment notes. This record helps your lawyer demonstrate the extent of your injuries and connect them to your accident, potentially increasing your settlement.

If you’ve filed a personal injury claim, we’ll coordinate with your lawyer to create a medical lien agreement so that you can receive treatment without out-of-pocket costs.

Call an Emergency Dentist in San Francisco Today

After an accident, don’t put off dental care. When you see an emergency dentist in San Francisco, we can quickly restore any missing or broken teeth, preventing future damage. To make the process easier, we partner with personal injury attorneys to arrange medical lien agreements, so you can receive urgent treatment without upfront costs. Plus, with these services, we will document your injuries and required treatments to help strengthen your case.

At San Francisco East Bay Mini Dental Implants, we serve emergency patients weekly. We’re prepared to treat patients with dental damage resulting from a wide range of accidents.
