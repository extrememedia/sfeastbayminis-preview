Snap Into Confidence With Snap-In Dentures

Published January 17, 2025By Dr. Rose Magno

Imagine a loose, flickering lightbulb that struggles to provide the light you need. If it’s not screwed in tightly, you take the time to secure it, ensuring it shines brightly and consistently. The same principle applies to your dentures. Just as a loose light bulb fails to work until it’s secured, loose dentures can fail to provide the support you need for daily activities like eating and speaking. To make sure they stay in place, they often require adjustments and repairs—much like that loose lightbulb. Moreover, loose dentures can negatively impact your self-esteem. Dentures need to stay locked in your mouth to enjoy a confident, comfortable fit. One way to accomplish this is with snap-in dentures. These innovative implant dentures keep your teeth in place, allowing you to smile and speak confidently!

What Are Traditional Dentures?

With the denture market expanding, it’s important to understand the different types of dentures and which option may be best for you. Traditional dentures fall into two categories: full or partial. They can either replace a full set of teeth or several missing teeth. Dental lab technicians make dentures out of acrylic resin or porcelain. They customize each set to match the natural color of your teeth and gums. Traditional dentures rest directly on your gums and rely on suction, aided by your saliva, to stay in place. Some patients also use adhesives for additional security.

Traditional dentures are convenient for those looking to restore their smile with a simple, removable solution. However, they do come with some drawbacks.

Many people report difficulty eating or speaking with their new dentures. Patients have also said traditional dentures can be uncomfortable and take a while to get used to. On top of this, dentures can loosen over time, causing them to shift and even fall out.

While this traditional method is one way to restore your smile, it isn’t the only option. Unlike traditional dentures, which can slip out of place, snap-in dentures provide a secure, reliable alternative to keep your false teeth in their proper position.

What Are Snap-In Dentures?

Like traditional dentures, snap-in dentures are removable. However, they’re much more secure. They snap in place with the help of 4-8 mini dental implants. With this system, you don’t have to worry about your dentures falling out or wearing sticky adhesives to keep them still.

Mini dental implants are a less invasive alternative to traditional implants. This type of implant is less than 3 mm wide—about the width of a toothpick. Dr. Rose Magno permanently secures them in the jawbone to create a solid foundation for your false teeth.

Because of their small size, mini implants don’t normally require a bone graft like traditional implants do. Their size also makes mini implants a great choice for those who have experienced bone loss in their jaws.

Once Dr. Magno inserts your mini implants into the jawbone, she’ll attach your denture, snapping it in place.

What Are the Benefits of Snap-In Dentures?

Snap-in dentures offer unparalleled stability. Because they stay in place, you can enjoy all your favorite foods like juicy steak, corn on the cob, or crunchy apples. Aside from eating freely and providing a secure fit, implant-retained dentures look and feel natural. They also offer comfort and confidence that make you feel better about smiling.

Caring for your snap-in dentures is relatively simple. Just remove them to clean both the denture and implants. Make sure you rinse and brush your dentures and dental implants with a soft-bristled toothbrush. A water flosser can also be a helpful addition to your oral hygiene tools.

In addition to their immediate functional benefits, snap-in dentures also contribute to long-term bone health. Like natural tooth roots, dental implants stimulate the jawbone. This stimulation keeps the bone strong and prevents bone loss that normally occurs with tooth loss. This occurs because the dental implants serve as artificial roots and fuse with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration.

Unlike traditional denture options, snap-in dentures stay firmly in place, giving you peace of mind throughout the day. This hassle-free solution simplifies your life while boosting your confidence.
