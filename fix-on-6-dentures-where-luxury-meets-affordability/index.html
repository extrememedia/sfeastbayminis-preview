Fix-on-6 Dentures: Where Luxury Meets Affordability [Video Inside]

Published May 4, 2026By Dr. Rose Magno

Replacing a full arch of missing teeth can be life-changing, but the cost and complexity of treatment often hold patients back. Mini dental implants can make full-arch replacement less invasive and more affordable than traditional implant options. If you’re ready to replace missing teeth, permanent roundhouse bridges are among the most high-end solutions, offering realistic restorations that can improve your quality of life. But even with the cost benefits of mini implants, patients often find the price point is still a significant investment. Fix-on-6, a semi-permanent roundhouse bridge, meets you in the middle, closely resembling your natural teeth at a more affordable price compared to fully permanent options.

Every day, Dr. Magno sees patients who struggle to put themselves first. She wants to make it easier for you to treat yourself to a full-arch replacement that empowers you to smile. That’s why she proudly offers Fix-on-6®.

The Highs and Lows of Permanent Roundhouse Bridges

The permanent roundhouse bridge is often considered one of the most realistic-looking options for replacing all your missing teeth. This implant-supported prosthetic is permanently cemented onto dental implants. Usually, it is anchored to 10-12 mini dental implants or 4

traditional implants

on the top or bottom arch. The prosthetic never comes out, which better reflects what it feels like to have your natural teeth compared to removable dentures.

The roundhouse bridge itself is made of durable, dazzling zirconia that withstands normal biting pressure. Once secured onto dental implants, you’ll be able to use your prosthetic teeth to grind down food with a functional chewing and biting motion. Dental implants help stimulate the jawbone, which may slow the bone loss that commonly follows tooth loss.

Permanent dentures are a luxurious way to replace missing teeth, lasting 20 years or more with maintenance. They’re also among the most expensive ways to replace all your missing teeth. If price is a barrier, don’t give up on your dream smile. Consider Fix-on-6®, the semi-permanent alternative offered at a more affordable cost.

Fix-on-6: The Affordable Solution for a Fixed Denture

Fix-on-6® is a roundhouse bridge option that attaches to 6-10 mini implants in the upper or lower arch. This alternative is considered semi-permanent because you do not remove it yourself. Instead, Dr. Magno can remove it during office visits for professional maintenance and deep cleaning.

From your experience as a patient, Fix-on-6 will look and feel very similar to a premium denture. In fact, the “teeth” components are made out of the same high-quality zirconia as the permanent roundhouse bridge. Sometimes, if your jaws have changed shape, we can design your semi-permanent dentures to include some porcelain “gums” to support the shape of your face. With proper care and maintenance, these restorations may last many years, and some can last 20 years or longer.

Although it has a similar impact, this alternative typically costs less than a fully permanent roundhouse bridge. By using mini dental implants in a streamlined, semi-permanent design, Fix-on-6 offers fixed, zirconia teeth at a more accessible price point. And by supporting Fix-on-6 with mini dental implants, we’re using a more affordable option than traditional implants. Because mini dental implants are smaller in diameter, they can often be placed with a less invasive approach and may reduce the need for bone grafting.

Even with these benefits, our practice makes it even easier to manage the cost of care, helping you afford your tooth replacement with helpful financing options.

Financing Options for Fix-on-6

The semi-permanent roundhouse bridge improves on affordability, but we understand that you might still be wondering how to best manage the investment in your smile. Dr. Magno’s practice works with multiple financing companies to bring your ideal smile within reach. These options allow you to take out a loan or create a payment plan before treatment begins. Then you’ll pay off your care in manageable monthly installments, often as low as $100 to $200.

Our financing solutions include loans, lines of credit, and buy-now-pay-later options. When you’re ready to commit to the Fix-on-6® smile restoration, our staff will help you fill out the financing application right in our office. If you have a low credit score, you can often apply with a co-signer to increase your chances of approval. Our practice even provides in-office financing, another way to manage the expense.

Cost doesn’t have to keep you from exploring your options. Our practice reduces costs by offering Fix-on-6 and helps you manage the remaining expenses with convenient payment plans.
