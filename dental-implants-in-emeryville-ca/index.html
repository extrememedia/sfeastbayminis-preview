What Is a Dental Implant?

Dental implants are medical-grade titanium posts positioned in the jawbone to replace missing teeth. They act as a sturdy base for restorations like crowns, bridges, or dentures.

Unlike conventional dentures, which rest on the gums, or traditional bridges that anchor onto surrounding teeth, implants integrate directly with the bone. This process, called osseointegration, creates a stable and durable foundation, allowing the replacement teeth to function similarly to natural teeth.

Additionally, since dental implants are essentially artificial tooth roots, they replicate the function of your missing roots. That may not be your immediate concern, but it’s incredibly important. Below the surface of your gums, your natural roots are embedded in your jawbone. The roots don’t just keep your teeth in place—they also maintain your bone health. When you eat, the roots transmit your chewing forces onto the jaw, and this pressure allows for proper blood flow. The blood carries the nutrients the bone needs to stay strong and maintain its mass. This means that replacing your missing teeth with dental implants also supports your long-term bone health.

The Benefits of Dental Implants

In addition to replacing missing teeth and preserving the jawbone, dental implants provide several other benefits:

  • Durability: Titanium implants are long-lasting and reliable solutions for tooth replacement. With proper care, they can even last a lifetime.
  • Functionality: Implants restore your ability to chew and speak naturally, enhancing overall oral function. Their secure fit allows you to enjoy your favorite goods and speak confidently, knowing your replacement teeth will stay firmly in place.
  • Smile aesthetics: The appearance of implant-supported restorations closely mimics natural teeth, providing a seamless and natural look.
  • Facial aesthetics: By preserving the jawbone and preventing bone loss, dental implants help maintain your natural facial structure. This sets them apart from other options like traditional dentures, which can lead to changes in appearance over time.
  • Convenience: Unlike removable dentures, implants eliminate the need for adhesives and do not require daily removal, simplifying oral care routines.

Mini Dental Implants vs. Traditional Dental Implants

A traditional dental implant was the first type of dental implant designed to replace a missing tooth from the root to the crown. These implants are made up of a few pieces:

  • The implant post, measuring about 5 mm wide
  • The abutment (a connector piece)
  • The crown, bridge, or denture

Because the post is so wide (about the width of a pencil eraser) and there are multiple components, getting even one traditional implant is a lengthy process that involves multiple appointments. Additionally, because the implant is quite large, many people are not eligible for the treatment. This is typically because they don’t have sufficient jawbone space for the implant or cannot undergo oral surgery.

While traditional implants are still reliable, modern advancements like mini dental implants offer even better solutions. It’s similar to upgrading from a flip phone to a smartphone or trading in a tube TV for a sleek Smart TV.

The sleek design of modern technologies is reflected in the mini dental implant design. At half the size of traditional dental implants, mini implants are more suitable for patients with limited bone density or those who need a less invasive procedure. They’re also a single piece with an integrated abutment. This slimmer, one-piece design allows Dr. Magno to place a mini implant using a much quicker and less invasive procedure. The result?

  • Less pain
  • Less bleeding
  • Less time spent in a dental chair
  • Less healing times
  • Less impact on your wallet

Because mini implant treatment is so streamlined and efficient, it’s about half the cost of a traditional implant procedure. This cost savings makes mini implants an attractive choice for those on a budget!

The Dental Implant Procedure

The dental implant process begins with an initial consultation, where Dr. Rose Magno assesses your oral health and bone structure through an exam and detailed 3D imaging. Based on this evaluation, Dr. Magno will develop your customized treatment plan to address your specific needs and goals while keeping your budget in mind.

With traditional dental implant surgery, the metal post is placed into the jawbone to act as a prosthetic tooth root. This process requires a healing period of several months, during which the implant integrates with the bone. After the treatment site has healed, an abutment is attached to the post, and a custom restoration is attached to the abutment. This multi-step surgery creates a strong foundation for replacement teeth, but it can be time-consuming and may not be suitable for everyone due to the extended healing period and bone density requirements.

Thanks to the unique features of mini dental implants, Dr. Magno performs the mini implant procedure in a much more streamlined and minimally invasive way. First, she gently creates a tiny opening in the gums—no incision needed. Then, she’ll guide the small implant into place. Because mini implants are exceptionally stable as soon as Dr. Magno anchors them in the bone, she can immediately attach a temporary restoration. With mini implants, no long healing periods are needed. When your final restoration has been fabricated in the dental lab, we’ll simply replace the temporary one.

Dr. Magno’s precise and efficient technique delivers stability, comfort, immediate results, and a faster recovery for her patients.

Frequently asked questions

Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

Individuals considering dental implants must meet certain criteria to ensure the best possible results. General health matters since some medical conditions or medications can affect how well implants work. Also, non-smokers tend to be better candidates because smoking can interfere with the healing process and how well the implants integrate.

Candidates should also have healthy gums and sufficient bone density to support the implants. But here’s the exciting part—if you’ve been told you’re not a candidate for traditional implants because of your jawbone, mini dental implants might still be an option for you!

How do I care for dental implants?

Taking care of your dental implants is pretty simple, but it makes a big difference! Regular brushing and flossing keep your implants and gums clean and healthy, helping to prevent infections. Don’t skip those routine dental visits either—they’re key for cleanings and checkups so Dr. Magno can keep an eye on how your implants are holding up. If you ever notice discomfort or something feels off, let us know right away. Staying on top of things will keep your implants looking great and working perfectly for years to come!

How much do dental implants cost?

While we’d love to give you one answer, the truth is that it varies based on a few factors, like how many implants you need, the type of restoration (crown, bridge, or denture), and the materials used to create it.

That said, here’s the good news: Mini dental implants cost about half as much as traditional implants. They’re a more budget-friendly option that doesn’t sacrifice quality or durability. To get an accurate estimate, Dr. Magno will need to evaluate your unique needs and goals during a consultation. Together, we’ll create a treatment plan that works for your smile and your budget!
