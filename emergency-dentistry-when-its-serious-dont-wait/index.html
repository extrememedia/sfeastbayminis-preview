In Vladimir Nabokov’s novel Pnin, the title character tires of his constant need for emergency dentistry. He finally opts to have all his teeth removed and to be fitted with a pair of dentures. Following that procedure, he tells anyone who will listen that he’s never felt better in his life. It’s the only thing he does in the novel with any real energy.

Of course, it’s hard to act energetically when you’re experiencing a dental emergency. So it’s a good thing that emergency dentists like Dr. Rose Magno are there for you when you need them. Dr. Magno (DDS, FAACD, FICOI, DIAMDI) is an expert at treating urgent dental issues such as: 

  • Bleeding
  • Tooth damage
  • Swelling
  • Tooth pain

The goal of emergency dentistry is to restore your comfort and oral function. If you’re experiencing the above symptoms or think you may need urgent care, contact Dr. Rose Magno immediately for emergency dental treatments. 

What Qualifies as a Dental Emergency?

In the U.S., 2 million people seek help for dental emergencies in a hospital emergency room annually. This places an undue burden on the hospital system, without getting patients the relief they seek. In most cases, it’s better to seek emergency dentistry treatment from a qualified dentist.

Some people might doubt that what they’re experiencing is a “true” dental emergency. But there’s no reason to suffer out of politeness. Common dental emergencies include: 

  • Abscesses or infections: An abscess occurs when pus collects in your mouth. It causes painful swelling, throbbing, and even fever.
  • Broken prosthetics: If your bridge, veneer, implant, or dentures become damaged, you may face more than just cosmetic concerns. You can experience real pain. A damaged restoration can alter your bite, making chewing uncomfortable, and may expose sensitive areas of your mouth, causing increased discomfort.
  • Chipped or cracked teeth: A chipped tooth can be out-and-out painful. It can also expose the inner part of the tooth—known as the pulp—to potential infection, leading to greater pain, tooth loss, and a need for complex tooth-and-gum procedures. 
  • Knocked-out teeth: If a knocked-out tooth won’t fit back in its socket, place it in either milk or saline and bring it to an emergency dentist. There’s a chance Dr. Magno can save it if you get to her quickly.
  • Severe dental pain: Severe dental pain of any kind can indicate a serious underlying problem. Aside from that, it hurts! And you don’t have to live with the misery of dental pain. See Dr. Magno as soon as possible for the best possible outcomes.

If you experience any of the above symptoms, contact Dr. Magno right away. Putting off emergency dentistry can lead to complications down the road, such as nerve damage, infection spread, and even tooth loss.

What Are Some Emergency Dentistry Techniques?

At San Francisco East Bay Mini Dental Implants, Dr. Magno offers a variety of emergency dental procedures for patients in need. These include:

Dental Crowns

Emergency dentists often use dental crowns to fix chipped or cracked teeth. They both protect what remains of your natural teeth and restore their natural function by “capping” the visible portion of the tooth.

Dental Bonding

Dental bonding is a good option for tooth damage that isn’t severe enough to warrant a crown. Bonding utilizes a resin that Dr. Magno can match to the color of your natural teeth. This is a great option for those who are cost-conscious, as dental bonding is an affordable treatment that can last up to 10 years. 

Veneers

Emergency dentists affix veneers—shells, basically—to the front of damaged teeth. Most commonly, veneers are used for your top teeth—those most visible when you smile. 

Extraction

If a tooth is damaged beyond repair, or if it’s severely infected, Dr. Magno may recommend complete tooth extraction. She can then install a prosthetic where the tooth used to be.

Mini Dental Implants and Their Place in Emergency Dentistry

Mini dental implants have an increasingly valuable place in emergency dentistry due to their versatility, effectiveness, and affordability.

Dr. Magno gently positions these small titanium posts into your jawbone. Once secured, she attaches a custom prosthetic, such as a crown, bridge, or denture, to the post. This versatile solution can replace a single tooth, multiple teeth, or even an entire set of teeth in your upper or lower jaw.

Those needing to replace all their teeth can opt for implant dentures. Unlike traditional dentures, which are prone to sliding and loosening, implant dentures are anchored to your jawbone. This gives you a more reliable bite without the need for dental adhesives or periodic adjustments.

While tooth loss can be life-changing, mini dental implants offer a stable, comfortable, and long-lasting solution to restore your smile!
