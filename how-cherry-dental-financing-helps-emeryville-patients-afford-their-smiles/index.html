Published July 27, 2026By Dr. Rose Magno

Dr. Magno has been applying advanced techniques to renew smiles with veneers, mini implants, and clear aligners, among many other cutting-edge treatment options. Even though a beautiful dental makeover can be life-changing for patients, cost often holds them back. Our practice thinks you should be able to afford a more beautiful, healthier set of teeth. That’s why we work with Cherry, a dental financing provider with high approval rates and flexible financing options.

With Cherry and a variety of other payment plans, you can afford a smile makeover sooner than you think!

Cherry Dental Financing Explained

Our practice accepts different dental insurance plans. However, patients often find they have outstanding costs remaining, especially for transformative dental procedures considered “elective.” Payment plan options like Cherry help cover those remaining costs and turn what feels like a large investment into something more manageable.

If you know you’d benefit from a smile makeover, but the financial commitment is causing you stress, Cherry can help ease that tension by making it possible to start treatment sooner. Once your care is complete, you can enjoy your dental implants, Invisalign, crowns, or cosmetic work. Over time, you can slowly pay off your care in smaller bi‑weekly or monthly installments.

Below are some key features of Cherry that help patients bring their smile within reach.

No Hard Credit Check

Cherry uses a soft credit check during the application process. This means that applying for Cherry doesn’t hurt your credit score. There’s no harm in checking what you qualify for.

True 0% APR Options

Cherry offers “true 0% interest” promotional plans. This means that if patients make the required payments on time, they finish the promotional period with no interest and no remaining balance. Cherry does not retroactively apply interest due to a single late payment, so you won’t be surprised by a much larger balance months down the line.

Transparent Pricing

Before you commit to a Cherry payment plan, you’ll see your total cost, term length, APR (if applicable), and exact payment amount.

Fast Application and High Approval Rates

It only takes about a minute to apply on a phone or tablet, and in most cases, you’ll receive a decision instantly. Cherry is known for approving more patients, including those with lower credit scores.

Flexible Terms and Coverage Amounts

Depending on your eligibility, you can access financing of up to $50,000-$65,000 for your smile upgrade. Plans range from short pay‑in‑4 arrangements to long‑term installments of up to 60 months. Whether you want to finance a simple procedure or an entire full-arch restoration, Cherry is certainly an option worth considering.

For these reasons, Cherry is a leading financing choice among many patients. Cherry determines specific approval decisions, credit limits, terms, and APRs, which may vary based on your individual credit profile and other underwriting criteria.

Other Payment Plan Options To Explore

We know that Cherry tends to be a strong financing option. However, at our practice, we like to offer patients as many financing options as possible. You can choose between a number of other credit cards and loans as well. Here are some alternatives to Cherry that you can also use for dental financing at our practice:

  • Happen (Formerly known as Lending Club): This option is a traditional fixed‑term loan with clear monthly payments. You can be approved for large limits up to about $65,000. You can also pre‑qualify with no impact on your credit score.
  • Alphaeon Credit: This reusable healthcare credit card offers a credit limit of up to $25,000. You can reuse the same card for future dental and cosmetic treatments at participating providers.
  • United Credit: This is a single application process that serves as a payment plan matchmaker of sorts. You could be matched with multiple lenders based on your credit score and whether or not you have a co-signer. Even if your credit isn’t perfect, you could still find an option that fits your needs.
  • Healthcare Financing Direct: If you’ve struggled with approvals for other options, this is a pay‑over‑time plan from a platform with near-universal approvals and customized programs for all credit types.

The bottom line is clear. If you feel you can’t afford a dental makeover, Dr. Magno’s practice offers financing options that suit your needs.

Learn More About Financing at a Consultation

If you’re wondering how you’ll be able to afford the smile of your dreams, our practice makes it easier with Cherry dental financing. This buy-now, pay-later option is easy to apply for. Cherry payment plans come with clear, transparent expectations about how much their dental makeover will cost and how long they’ll be paying off the loan. While Cherry is a popular option with high approval rates, our practice wants to give you as many choices as possible. We work with a variety of other financing providers. The goal is to give you the best chance of finding a dental payment plan that makes sense for you.

Dr. Magno doesn’t want financing concerns to stop you from accessing a smile that can change your life. Learn if Cherry or another payment plan option can help you afford your dental makeover. Text or call Dr. Magno at 510-591-0091 to schedule an affordable consultation.
