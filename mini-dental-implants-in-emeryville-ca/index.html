What Are Mini Dental Implants?

Mini dental implants are a breakthrough in modern dentistry, offering patients a secure, long-lasting, and less invasive solution for replacing missing teeth. Unlike traditional dental implants—which require more extensive surgery—mini implants use a simplified, comfortable technique that’s ideal for patients who may not be candidates for full-size implants.

Before mini implants became available over 20 years ago, patients often needed multiple surgeries and longer healing time. Traditional implants are typically around 5 mm in diameter (similar to a pencil eraser) and require two surgeries to filet open the gums and create a larger hole in the jawbone. This process often excluded patients with thinner bone or health conditions that make surgery risky.

Why Choose Mini Dental Implants?

Mini dental implants offer a modern alternative. These narrow implants—less than 3 mm wide—are placed using a gentle, minimally invasive method. Dr. Rose Magno places them through the gum tissue with precision, creating only a tiny channel to comfortably guide the implant into place. Because the implants are made of a single piece and anchor securely on the same day, there’s no need for stitches, bone grafts, or long wait times.

Dr. Magno and her team at San Francisco East Bay Mini Dental Implants are proud to be part of the Mini Dental Implant Centers of America (MDICA)—a national network of trusted doctors providing this innovative care.

What To Expect During the Mini Implant Process

  1. Personal Consultation:
    You’ll meet with Dr. Magno to talk about your dental concerns and goals. She’ll perform a thorough oral exam and take digital X-rays to assess your bone structure and overall oral health. If you’re a good candidate, she’ll create a custom treatment plan tailored to your smile and discuss your options.
  2. Comfortable Placement:
    Using a gentle and precise method, Dr. Magno will create a small opening in the gum and bone to place the mini implant. Because of their slim design and secure fit, she can often attach a temporary tooth or denture right away—so you leave your appointment with a complete, functional smile.
  3. Final Restoration:
    After about two weeks, you’ll return for your final restoration. Dr. Magno will replace the temporary tooth with your permanent one, beautifully matched to your natural smile for lasting comfort and confidence.

Benefits of Mini Dental Implants

Mini implants offer many advantages to patients seeking long-lasting and dependable tooth replacement:

  • Minimally Invasive: No stitches, cutting, or lengthy surgery—just a gentle, same-day procedure.
  • Faster Healing: Most patients recover quickly with minimal discomfort and only need over-the-counter medication.
  • More Affordable: Mini implants cost less than traditional implants due to their streamlined design and efficient placement process.
  • Broader Eligibility: Perfect for patients with bone loss, medical conditions, or dental anxiety.
  • Immediate Results: Walk out with teeth the same day—no waiting for months!
  • Versatile: Mini implants can support single teeth, multiple teeth, or even full dentures.

Frequently asked questions

How much do mini dental implants cost?

If you’re looking for affordable dental implants, mini implants are the answer. They’re about half the price of conventional dental implants!

The exact cost of your treatment will depend on the number of implants you need as well as your restoration. For instance, a single implant with a crown will cost less than an implant denture.

Cost transparency is important to us, so we’ll be sure to outline the cost of your dental care in your personalized treatment plan.

Do you offer financing for mini dental implants?

Yes! We believe that everyone deserves access to life-changing dental treatments. Because dental insurance doesn’t often cover dental implants, that can seem impossible to some patients. However, with our flexible financing options, affording new teeth has never been simpler. By partnering with third-party lending companies, we give patients the ability to get their treatment now and pay for it later. With fixed monthly payments and the flexibility to choose a loan term that works for you, you’ll be able to take control of your oral health without straining your wallet.

How long do mini dental implants last?

Like conventional dental implants, mini dental implants are made of durable titanium. This metal is preferred because of its proven track record of osseointegration (the fusion of the implant with your bone). The long-term success of titanium implants means that you can count on your mini dental implants to last for many years. With proper care, your implants can last a lifetime!

Just like your natural teeth, it’s important to care for your dental implants with strong brushing and flossing habits. Attending your regular six-month visits is also crucial. These checkups allow us to clean your implants meticulously and evaluate their condition.
