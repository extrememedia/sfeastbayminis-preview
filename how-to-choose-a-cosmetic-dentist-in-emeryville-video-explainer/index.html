If you’re ready to make changes to your smile, choosing the right cosmetic dentist is key to achieving results that will make you excited to show off your teeth again. When weighing your options, consider the treatments each office offers and the experience a dentist brings to the table. You should also keep an eye out for positive patient reviews and testimonials.

Dr. Rose Magno is a leading cosmetic dentist in Emeryville with over 25 years of experience enhancing smiles and a focus on natural-looking results.

Considering a Cosmetic Dentist? Here’s What To Look For

A great smile can change the way you show up in the world. However, not every cosmetic dentist will get you there the same way. Before you commit to a provider, it helps to know exactly what to look for, from the treatments they offer to the credentials behind their work. Here’s what you should prioritize when choosing a cosmetic dentist.

Consider Treatments Offered

One of the most important things to consider when choosing a cosmetic dentist is which services they actually offer. There’s a wide range of procedures available to improve certain characteristics of your smile. Here are some of the most common ones, along with the concerns they can address:

  • Cosmetic dental implants are small titanium posts placed in the jawbone to support replacement teeth.
  • Veneers are thin porcelain shells that cover the front of the teeth, adjusting shape, size, and color.
  • Invisalign® is a series of clear aligners that gradually straighten teeth without braces.
  • Dental crowns are tooth-shaped caps that restore strength and function after a tooth is damaged by decay.

If you’re interested in improving any of these aspects of your smile, you should consider choosing Dr. Magno. She offers every single one of these services with an eye for natural-looking results. Her goal is to provide results that look natural and support each patient’s individual smile goals.

Review Testimonials and Before-and-After Photos

Before choosing a cosmetic dentist, check patient testimonials and explore before-and-after photos to see the kind of results you should expect. Photos can give you a better sense of the dentist’s work and aesthetic approach. Moreover, reading real patient reviews tells you how people felt about their experience from consultation through the final result.

Dr. Magno has dozens of positive patient reviews, including video testimonials, along with a smile gallery of before-and-after photos available right on her website. Looking through these can help you get a feel for her approach and the natural-looking results her patients walk away with.

Review Credentials and Experience

You should also check the dentist’s credentials and expertise. A dentist dedicated to their craft should have relevant credentials and a commitment to continuing education.

Dr. Magno has practiced cosmetic dentistry for over 25 years. She is a Fellow of the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry (FAACD). This distinction reflects her advanced training and proven skill set in smile aesthetics. She has furthered her cosmetic expertise through continuing education at the Las Vegas Institute (LVI), including studies in advanced cosmetic and laser dentistry. She is also a Diplomate of the International Academy of Mini Dental Implants. Lastly, her practice has been exclusively designated as Emeryville’s Mini Dental Implant Center of America.

Dr. Magno says she loves her job because it allows her to help people and make a real difference in their lives.

Trusting a cosmetic dentist with your smile is an important decision. However, when you focus on the factors that matter most when choosing a cosmetic dentist, you can feel confident making the decision that’s best for your smile. Dr. Magno offers a full range of cosmetic treatments, backed by more than 25 years of experience and advanced training. When you’re ready to start showing off your smile again, pick up the phone and call her practice.
