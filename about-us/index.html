Meet the team

A caring team, led by Dr. Magno

Dr. Rose Magno leads every treatment plan. Her chairside and front-office team is with you from the first call to the final check.

Lead dentist

Dr. Rose Magno

DDS, FAACD, FICOI, DIAMDI

Dr. Rose Magno brings an exceptional educational background to her practice, with degrees from the University of California, Berkeley (UCB), and the University of the Pacific (UOP) Dental School in San Francisco. She has also completed advanced training with the Pacific Aesthetic Continuum (now the Hornbrook Group) and the Las Vegas Institute (LVI). Dr. Magno stays at the forefront of dental innovation, continuously enhancing her knowledge and skills to deliver state-of-the-art, personalized, comfortable, and hygienically superior care to every patient.

Read her full story

Dr. Magno holds memberships in esteemed organizations, including the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD), American Dental Association (ADA), California Dental Association (CDA), American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry (AACD), Alameda County Dental Society (ACDS), and Crown Council. Her dedication to excellence in cosmetic dentistry is reflected in her affiliations with prestigious groups such as the Las Vegas Institute (LVI) and PAC-LIVE.

Recognized for her expertise, Dr. Magno has been voted one of America’s Top Dentists and Cosmetic Dentists every year since 2006.

Team

Your care team

Scheduling, insurance and chairside care: Dr. Magno’s team takes care of the details.

  • Clar

    Administrative & Operations Team Member

    Read more

    Clar is our Administrative & Operations Team Member at SF East Bay Mini Dental Implants. She helps coordinate appointments, new-patient communications, insurance verification and claim follow-ups, billing documentation, patient chart preparation, and other important administrative details that help keep our office organized and our patients well supported.

    Behind the scenes, Clar also helps coordinate Team communications, follow-ups, and office workflows and protocols. With her focus on organization, accuracy, clear communication, and consistent follow-through, Clar helps our Team provide patients with a smooth, welcoming, and well-organized experience from their first contact with our office through their ongoing care.

  • Pauline

    Dental Assistant & Administrative Team Member

    Read more

    Pauline is our Dental Assistant & Administrative Team Member at SF East Bay Mini Dental Implants. As a working student and consistent President’s Lister at her school, Pauline successfully balances her education with her responsibilities at our dental office. Her dedication to both her studies and her work reflects her commitment to learning, responsibility, organization, and personal growth.

    Pauline assists Dr. Rose Magno and our Team with patient care and daily office operations, including dental assisting, scheduling appointments, verifying insurance benefits, following up on insurance claims, and assisting with patient accounts and collections. Her strong communication and organizational skills, positive attitude, and willingness to learn help our Team provide patients with a friendly, efficient, and well-organized dental experience.
