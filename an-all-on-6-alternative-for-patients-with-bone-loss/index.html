An All on 6 Alternative for Patients With Bone Loss

Published May 5, 2025By Dr. Rose Magno

Did you know tooth loss is a common issue in the United States? At least 178 million Americans are missing one or more teeth. It’s likely many of them are familiar with the struggles that follow. Shifting teeth, bone loss, and misalignment can happen. If left untreated, you may even experience additional tooth loss. Daily activities that were once enjoyable, such as eating and speaking, are now challenging. Chewing your food seems to take longer, and you may notice changes in your speech pattern. This can make you feel insecure and affect your overall mood. The good news is that there are tooth replacement options to restore your health and your smile. One option is All on 6, a technique that uses traditional dental implants. However, it’s not an option foreveryone.

Fix-on-Six®, on the other hand, is a more accessible alternative. If you’ve never heard of these treatments before, Dr. Rose Magno will equip you with the knowledge necessary to make an informed decision. With her guidance and expertise, you’ll be well on your way to a better smile and optimal oral health!

All on 6 vs. Fix-on-Six: A 101 Guide

The differences between All on 6 and Fix-on-Six lie in the technique and the size of the dental implants. There are two categories to consider: traditional and mini dental implants. Conventional implants average 5 mm wide and have a two-piece design. It features an implant post and abutment, which is the connector between the post and your false teeth. These larger implants contribute to a lengthy, complex procedure. It starts with an incision made in your gums to expose the underlying bone. Then, drilling takes place to make room for the implant post. Afterward, the site is stitched up to heal. The abutment is not attached during this step, so you will need to return for another appointment. This means you’ll be without teeth for some time during your treatment. Overall, the process spans over several months.

Not everyone is eligible for traditional dental implants. Because they are bigger, you need enough bone to support them. Otherwise, you’ll require a bone graft, which is an additional step and procedure. All on 6 uses six traditional dental implants to support an entire row of teeth. This option is inaccessible for patients with significant bone loss. Fix-on-Six is a reliable and effective alternative. This innovative technique uses 6 to 10 mini dental implants, making it more accessible to a wider range of people.

Mini dental implants are less than 3 mm wide, significantly smaller than traditional implants. Their smaller size allows Dr. Magno to anchor them securely in areas with less bone volume, eliminating the need for a bone graft. These slim implants offer a simpler and less invasive procedure, resulting in less discomfort and a quicker recovery compared to traditional implants.

The Benefits of Fix-on-Six

Fix-on-Six offers several advantages over all on 6. Because Fix-on-Six uses six or more mini implants, it provides excellent stability, ensuring the zirconia bridge—a row of false teeth—stays securely in place. It also costs half the price of traditional implant options because the procedure is less complex and the implants are smaller.

Because mini implants require a less invasive placement process and fewer appointments, you have a shorter overall treatment timeline. Recovery is also quicker, and oftentimes, D. Magno can place your false teeth on the same day. You can also use your teeth immediately. Thereby, mini implants restore oral function faster than traditional implants.

Implants help prevent bone loss that occurs when teeth are missing. They maintain facial structure and a more youthful appearance. The key is in the titanium—a medical-grade metal alloy used for implants. This biocompatible material fuses seamlessly with your jawbone, creating an immensely strong and stable foundation for false teeth. The implants are built to endure and can last a lifetime with proper oral care and hygiene.

These advantages are ideal for all patients who seek an effective and natural-looking solution. The procedure is straightforward and contributes to better oral health.

Although tooth loss is a common problem, modern dentistry provides innovative solutions. Today, patients don’t have to struggle with oral health issues associated with tooth loss. Dr Magno offers viable options that suit many patients’ needs!
