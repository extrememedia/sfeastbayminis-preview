Signs You May Need a Tooth Extracted

Dr. Magno will recommend removing your tooth if it’s beyond saving. Common reasons for tooth extraction include:

  • Dental trauma: If your tooth breaks, chips, or cracks due to physical injury, we may need to extract it. Damaged teeth have an increased risk of infection and are extra sensitive to temperature and chewing pressure.
  • Tooth decay and infection: When enamel wears down, bacteria can create deep cavities that compromise your tooth structure. They can also penetrate the dentin layer beneath and reach the core of your tooth. This is painful, as the inner chamber of your tooth houses dental pulp, which includes nerves, blood vessels, and connective tissues. If your tooth can’t be restored with a root canal, Dr. Magno will extract it to prevent the spread of infection.
  • Periodontal (gum) disease: This bacterial infection destroys the gums and bone when left untreated. While various treatments are available, advanced gum disease may require tooth extraction.
  • Wisdom teeth: A staggering 85% of people need to have their wisdom teeth extracted in their lifetime. When these third molars come in, your smile doesn’t always have room for them. This can cause overcrowding, discomfort, and pain. Dr. Magno will extract troublesome wisdom teeth to provide pain relief. However, if your tooth is impacted, she may refer you to an expert on wisdom teeth and follow up with you after the procedure.

Dr. Magno has extensive experience extracting teeth and caring for the smiles of patients like you. Whether you have a dental emergency or need an extraction for another reason, we provide compassionate care you can trust!

The Tooth Extraction Process

We generally categorize dental extractions into two main types, depending on the tooth’s position and condition.

Simple Extraction

This extraction method is straightforward, as the tooth needing extraction is visible and accessible. First, Dr. Magno will numb the area using a local anesthetic, keeping the procedure painless. Then, using specialized tools, she will gently remove the tooth from its socket. After removing the tooth, Dr. Magno will clean and disinfect the socket, ensuring your extraction site is free of bacteria or infection.

After cleaning the socket, Dr. Magno will fill the space left by your tooth with bone graft material to support healing and jawbone regeneration. This material creates a stable foundation for future tooth replacement, ensuring you won’t have to leave a gap in your smile.

At the end of your procedure, Dr. Magno will provide antibiotics to aid the healing process.

Surgical Extraction

When the tooth is not easily accessible or visible above the gums, you may need a surgical extraction. Once again, Dr. Magno will numb the area around the tooth to keep you comfortably numb throughout the procedure. Traditionally, a surgical extraction requires an incision in the gums. However, Dr. Magno has invested in technology that uses ultrasonic vibrations to facilitate gentler extractions. With her Piezotome® cube, she can cut around the tooth without making a flap in the gums. This approach protects the surrounding soft tissues and preserves the bone. It is especially helpful for surgical extractions, where traditional methods can cause more trauma.

Recovery from a surgical extraction typically takes a little longer than a simple extraction. Dr. Magno will provide detailed instructions to help you care for your extraction site and ensure proper healing.

Why Replace Missing Teeth?

Tooth loss can significantly impact your oral health, so it’s crucial to replace missing teeth as soon as possible to avoid complications.

When you lose a tooth, you don’t only lose the part of its structure that you chew with, known as the crown. You also lose your tooth’s root—the bottom half of the tooth, which is embedded into the jawbone. This root stimulates the jawbone when you bite or chew, helping to maintain its health. Without this stimulation, your bone will lose mass and volume, resulting in a shrinking bone structure that can impact oral function, change the shape of your face, and make it difficult to get dental implants later.

To prevent the loss of jawbone, we recommend replacing the tooth with a dental implant as soon as possible. Dental implants are artificial tooth roots that support restorations and contribute to better long-term bone health. At San Francisco East Bay Mini Dental Implants, Dr. Magno uses mini dental implants to secure crowns, bridges, and dentures. These narrow-diameter implants are less than 3 millimeters wide and consist of one solid piece of titanium. These innovative implants offer many distinct advantages.

Due to their small design, we can place mini implants using a minimally invasive technique. This approach leads to less discomfort and quicker results. Traditional implants, which are about 5 millimeters in diameter, increase the procedure complexity, treatment timeline, and recovery period. These conventional implants also have various pieces that are placed in separate appointments over 3-6 months. In contrast, Dr. Magno can often extract a tooth and replace it with a mini dental implant in just one appointment!

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to heal from a tooth extraction?

While Dr. Magno prioritizes your comfort throughout the procedure, it’s normal to feel some discomfort in the days following your extraction. In most cases, this can be remedied with over-the-counter pain medication and an ice pack.

More complex extraction cases can extend the recovery period. In this case, you may be given medication to ease your pain, and you may need to rest for longer to give your surgical site time to heal.

How much does a tooth extraction cost?

Every tooth extraction case is different. Therefore, treatment costs depend on the complexity of your case and individual factors. At your consultation, Dr. Magno will discuss costs with you, and her experienced staff can help determine if your insurance covers your extraction.

If you need to finance your procedure, Dr. Magno works with third-party companies to provide flexible financing options. With these plans, you can split up your costs into manageable monthly payments. This option allows you to get the dental care you need without straining your finances!

What is the best aftercare for a tooth extraction?

Proper aftercare is key to healing after a tooth extraction. Rest and avoid strenuous activities for at least 24 hours, keeping your head elevated to reduce swelling. Use an ice pack on your cheek in short intervals during the first day. Take any prescribed pain medication as directed.

Stick to soft foods like yogurt or soup, and avoid hot, crunchy, or spicy items. Don’t use straws or smoke, as these can disrupt healing and lead to a painful condition called dry socket.

If you notice excessive pain, swelling, or signs of infection, contact Dr. Magno promptly. With proper care, you’ll recover quickly and minimize the risk of complications.
