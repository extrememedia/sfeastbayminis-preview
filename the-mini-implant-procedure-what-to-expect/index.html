Published December 8, 2025By Dr. Rose Magno

You’ve got a big event on your calendar, and it’s approaching fast. Whether it’s a wedding, reunion, or vacation, you’ve got a reason to smile. But if you have missing teeth, you could be feeling embarrassed about what your teeth will look like in photos. Fortunately, with a mini implant procedure, Dr. Magno can often restore smiles in time for your big event!

The mini dental implant procedure uses a less invasive approach than conventional implants, which makes it possible to place a crown, denture, or fixed bridge on the same day. You’ll be able to leave the office with a complete set of teeth. And you’ll be ready to smile for pictures as you capture life’s happiest moments.

But what can you expect from the mini implant procedure? The answer: convenience, cost-effective care, and a bright smile.

Steps of the Mini Implant Procedure

The mini implant procedure is a more straightforward process than you might imagine. From consultation to final restoration, Dr. Rose Magno works with you to identify your dental goals, gently place a mini implant, and leave the office with a temporary restoration until you receive your permanent tooth or teeth. Here’s what to expect:

  1. Personal consultation: First, you’ll meet with Dr. Magno to discuss your dental needs and goals. For instance, do you want to replace one tooth, or are you looking to stabilize loose dentures? At this visit, she will take 3D X-rays to review your oral health and get a better picture of your bone structure. She’ll also answer any questions you might have and then take impressions of your teeth.
  2. Placement: Next, Dr. Magno will create a small opening in the gum to place the mini implant, which is less than 3 mm wide. Most patients can get a temporary restoration and leave the office with a complete, aesthetically pleasing smile.
  3. Final restoration: In about two weeks, you’ll come back to the office and receive your permanent tooth or teeth.

Benefits of the Mini Implant Procedure Over Traditional Procedures

The mini implant procedure improves your experience as a patient compared to traditional implants.

Immediate loading, the process of getting a crown placed on the same day as a dental implant, was uncommon after the more invasive procedure to place traditional implants. These larger traditional implants require flap surgery, where the dentist cuts into the gums to access the bone underneath. It would take up to six months for the bone to fully heal before a crown could be safely secured.

The mini implant procedure, on the other hand, is minimally invasive. With reduced trauma to the gums and less time spent in the chair, your experience getting a mini dental implant minimizes discomfort and brings your smile back in a fraction of the time.

Patients tend to heal faster from the procedure, as well. Most patients recover between 24 to 48 hours with minimal swelling.

Despite their incredible efficiency, mini implants are built to last. They have a 95% success rate, which is comparable to traditional implants. With proper care, they can last a lifetime!

Who Is a Good Candidate for the Mini Implant Procedure?

The mini implant procedure makes dental implants accessible to more patients. Thanks to its innovative approach, the mini implant procedure overcomes three key barriers to a full smile: time, cost, and underlying health conditions.

For instance, if you’re short on time before your big gathering or event, the mini dental implant procedure makes it possible to restore your smile fast. Dr. Magno can work with you if you have an upcoming event and need your teeth restored quickly.

Because Dr. Magno can complete the procedure in fewer visits, it’s easier to fit tooth replacement into your busy schedule. Even if you don’t often put yourself first, this is a great way to do something meaningful for your own confidence!

Aside from time, you’ll also save on costs by restoring your smile in fewer visits. Mini implant procedures are less complex than conventional procedures. That means they can be done much faster, often in a single visit. They also minimize the need for additional procedures like bone grafting. The smaller mini implants themselves have fewer components, further driving down costs. Plus, dental financing options make it easier to afford your procedure, with monthly payments as low as $100 to $200.

The minimally invasive, non-surgical procedure also makes it possible for patients who can’t tolerate oral surgery to receive a mini dental implant. More patients are candidates for mini implants than for traditional dental implants. If you’ve been told you don’t have enough bone to get a conventional implant, you may still be able to achieve a complete smile with mini implants!

Take the Next Step to Mini Dental Implants

Whether you’re celebrating a milestone or just enjoying everyday moments with loved ones, you should feel proud to share your smile. Mini dental implants offer a faster, more affordable solution to replace missing teeth, so you can feel confident in any setting.
